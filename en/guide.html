How to move Samsung Health data to Apple Health on iPhone

Switched from a Samsung phone to an iPhone? Samsung Health can export everything it has recorded: sleep, heart rate, steps, workouts and more. This page shows how to get that export onto your iPhone and into Apple Health, in three steps.

1Export from Samsung Health

Open Samsung Health, go to its settings and find Download personal data.

The settings are behind the More options icon (three dots) and then Settings, or behind your profile picture, depending on the version.

3Bring the zip file to the iPhone and import it

Install Sampple on the iPhone and tap Receive from another phone: the iPhone shows a code, the Samsung phone scans it and sends the zip file over your Wi‑Fi. Or put the zip file in a cloud drive and choose it in the app. Sampple first shows what the file contains and what can be imported. Nothing is saved until you tap Import into Apple Health, and every import can be undone.

The free version imports the most recent 14 days of your export and all of your weight records. With the 1-Year Pass you can import all of your history, from every source the app supports, as often as you like, for one year. What the app learns to do during that year is included.
